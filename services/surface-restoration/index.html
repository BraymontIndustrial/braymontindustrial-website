Surface Restoration

Restore existing surfaces before the next finish goes on.

Braymont Industrial supports commercial and industrial restoration scopes where existing finishes, staining, rust or surface deterioration must be addressed before the next phase of work.

Restoration work can be coordinated with painting and protective coating scopes to provide a more complete surface-preparation and finishing solution.
